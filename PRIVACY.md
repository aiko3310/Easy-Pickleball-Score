# Privacy Policy — Easy Pickleball Score / 隱私權政策 — 簡單匹克球計分

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-09-24

## English
Easy Pickleball Score does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **Settings** (scoring format, points to win, etc.) are stored only on your watch.
- **Activity recording** (Player mode only): the app asks the watch to record a pickleball activity using the watch's built-in sensors (e.g. time, heart rate, calories). The activity is saved on your watch like any other Garmin activity and is synced to Garmin Connect only through your own Garmin account, under Garmin's privacy policy. The app itself never sends this data anywhere.
- The app has no network access, no analytics, and no advertising.

Questions: please open an issue at https://github.com/aiko3310/Easy-Pickleball-Score/issues

## 繁體中文
簡單匹克球計分不會蒐集、傳送或分享任何個人資料。

- **設定值**（賽制、幾分獲勝等）只儲存在你的手錶上。
- **活動錄製**（僅選手模式）：App 請手錶以內建感測器錄製一筆匹克球活動（例如時間、心率、卡路里）。活動和其他 Garmin 活動一樣儲存在手錶上，只會透過你自己的 Garmin 帳號同步到 Garmin Connect，並適用 Garmin 的隱私權政策。App 本身不會把這些資料傳到任何地方。
- App 沒有網路存取、沒有分析追蹤、沒有廣告。

有問題請至 https://github.com/aiko3310/Easy-Pickleball-Score/issues 提出。
